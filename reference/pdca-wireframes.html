Who makes your crown?

When your dentist takes an impression or a scan, a dental lab makes your crown, bridge or veneer. Here is what happens, and what you can ask.

Illustration: from impression to crown

What a dental lab does

  1. Your dentist

    prepares the tooth and takes an impression or a digital scan.

  2. The lab

    Our technicians design and make your restoration to your dentist's prescription.

  3. Your dentist

    fits it and checks your bite.

Why materials matter

We use authentic materials from reputable brands: Amann Girrbach zirconia, Ivoclar IPS e.max and VITA porcelain. You can trust what goes into your mouth.

Questions you can ask your dentist

  • Which lab makes my restoration?
  • What material is it made from, and which brand?
  • Should I visit the lab for shade taking?

Questions patients ask

Can I order a crown from the lab directly?

No. We make restorations to your dentist's prescription, so please speak to your dentist first.

How long does the lab take?

Usually 7 working days for simple cases and 10 for complex ones. Your dentist will confirm your appointment dates.

Can I choose the material?

Ask your dentist. They choose the material for your case and can tell you which brand it is.
